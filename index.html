My games

Both made under my own small studio, AU Games, from July 2026.

Flower Village

Cozy merge game · Android

Merge flowers, grow them in a greenhouse, craft products in the workshop and serve the villagers who come to your stall.

  • Designed the core loop: merge, grow, craft and serve.
  • Made 13 flowers, 13 villagers, 7 machines and 10 places, and translated the game into 7 languages.
  • Added daily tasks, two mini-games and a village rebuilding goal to bring players back every day.
  • Launched in the Philippines and Brazil after a closed test: 5,300+ installs, 25% store page conversion.
  • Ran 2 Google Ads campaigns: 4,600+ installs at 3.2 TRY each, below my target cost.
  • Tracked player steps with Firebase. Average play time: 38 minutes, about 2× similar games.

Sky Kitchen

Physics puzzle game · Android · Unity

Ingredients fall from the sky. Place kitchen tools to steer them into the right machines, and make shakes, juices and ice cream before time runs out.

  • Designed the puzzle idea: tools steer falling food, real physics decides the rest.
  • Built 50 levels with 6 tools, 4 machines and 15+ sky obstacles, adding a new idea every few levels.
  • Designed the game economy: coins, hearts, boosters, power-ups and rewarded ads.
  • Set up GameAnalytics events (start, win, fail, restart) on every level to find where players get stuck.
  • Wrote the game design document and released the game in November 2026.
  • Ran a 10-day Google Ads test in Serbia: 543 installs at 12.86 TRY each, below my target cost.
